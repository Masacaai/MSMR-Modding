## Hyper-Sense 1.0.0

Slow motion for Marvel's Spider-Man Remastered with a fading trail of Spider-Man after-images, set entirely from the game's own Settings screen.

> Back up your saves before you try any mod.

### What it does

- **Slow motion** on **T** or **D-pad Right**: five strengths from 0.5x down to 0.001x, and Spider-Man himself can run at 1x, 1.5x, 2x or 3x while the world crawls.
- **After-images**: copies of Spider-Man in his exact current pose, left behind while slow motion is on. They fade out and vanish the moment slow motion ends.
- **A HYPER-SENSE tab** in pause menu > SETTINGS with a row for everything: mode, look (the original, hologram, scanlines, red glow, flat grey, highlight, nine colours and RAINBOW), how long the copies last, how their opacity fades, starting opacity, spacing, flicker.
- **Presets**: a PRESET row applies one of the ini files in `Documents\Marvel's Spider-Man Remastered\HyperSense\Presets\`. Eleven ship with the mod (Flash, Reverse Flash, Hologram Trail, Matrix, Shadow Clones and more). Copy a file to make or share your own.
- **Experimental rows** (in their own section of the tab): clone drift, clone size over life, speed blur and speed streaks.

### Requirements

- Marvel's Spider-Man Remastered, **`Spider-Man.exe` version exactly 4.0630.0.0**. On any other build the mod refuses to touch the game.
- Overstrike 1.8.1 with **Enable .script support** and **Add '-scripts' to commandline.txt** on.
- Do not install it together with Quantum Break Renewed: both use the same keys and the same game fields.

### Known Issues

- The DISTORTION and FEAR looks crashed the game once (with a large burst of copies being removed just after slow motion ended). It has not been reproduced; if it happens to you, pick another look. No shipped preset uses them.
- At the slowest strength (0.001x) the game's clock barely moves, so the copies are held still more often than you might expect.
- Speed blur and speed streaks only show while Spider-Man is moving fast.
