## EndlessArena 0.2.0 beta 1

An endless-survival mode for Marvel's Spider-Man Remastered. Take a Manhattan dock circle to Ryker's Island and fight wave after wave.

> **This is a beta.** It has been played a lot by one person and works, but it is not bug-free (see below). Back up your saves before you try it.

### Requirements

- Marvel's Spider-Man Remastered, **`Spider-Man.exe` version exactly 4.0630.0.0**. Any other build: the mod refuses to touch the game. Developed and tested on the Steam version.
- [Overstrike](https://www.nexusmods.com/marvelsspidermanremastered/mods/4199) 1.8.1 with **Enable .script support** and **Add '-scripts' to commandline.txt** turned on.
  Overstrike's stock scripts proxy has a known issue; see the [setup notes](https://github.com/Masacaai/Spider-Man-Endless-Mode/blob/main/docs/GETTING_STARTED.md#2-overstrike-and-the-scripts-proxy).

### Install

1. Download **`EndlessArena.script`** below.
2. In Overstrike, add it to your mod list and press **Install mods**.
3. Launch the game, load a save, and go to a Manhattan dock circle. Stand on it to see your best run and goals; press the START prompt to travel to Ryker's, then walk into the yard to begin.

Your settings and records are kept in `Documents\Marvel's Spider-Man Remastered\EndlessMode\`, so reinstalling or updating Overstrike mods does not reset them.

To remove it: remove the mod in Overstrike and press Install mods again (you can also delete `scripts\EndlessMode*` from the game folder).

### What's new since the alpha

- **Campaign is now a roguelike.** You start each run with no suit power, mods or gadgets and earn them. After each wave you pick 1 of 3 rewards; after bosses you choose a suit power and may take a bittersweet **curse** (a strong upside with a real downside).
- **New boons:** Peter Tingle (the first hit each wave does nothing), Under the Machinery (tougher and self-healing when low), Black Suit (damage grows with a kill streak), Wheatcakes (heal after a boss wave), Slow Burn and Full Tank (Focus).
- **New curses:** Parker Luck, Rent Is Due, Clone Saga, Dry Spell and One-Punch Spider (everything goes down in one hit, including you).
- **Gadgets are rewards.** The gadgets start locked for the run and are offered as rewards from wave 2 on. Gadget-dependent perks (Resupply, Battle Tech, Scavenger, Metabolic Converter) only appear once you own a gadget.
- **Cleaner reward pool:** stealth, scanner and XP-only perks that do nothing in a fight are left out (switch it off with the "Skip useless powers" setting).
- **Debug mode** for the arena (see `docs/HOW_TO.md`) for trying things out by command.
- Enemies that get stuck in walls or unreachable spots are teleported out, and spawns now cycle around the whole prison.

### Things to know

- If an enemy is stuck inside a wall or in an unreachable area, move away from him and look elsewhere. He will be teleported out.
- Gadgets still look locked (red, 0/0) after you unlock them. They only look usable once you equip them for the first time.

### Known issues (see the [issue tracker](https://github.com/Masacaai/Spider-Man-Endless-Mode/issues))

- Occasional white cubes, floating heads or invisible enemies if the game runs out of model room; the mod caps how many enemy types it loads (`[run] max_types` in `Documents\Marvel's Spider-Man Remastered\EndlessMode\EndlessMode.ini`; lower it if you see this).
- Heavies/miniguns often stand around without engaging.
- Bodies sometimes stand up in a fight pose. Defeated bosses can freeze in a static pose.
- Results panels always show a Retry prompt next to Continue; that is how the game's panel works.

### Untested at release

- The reward-pool filter and gadget gating were built and installed late; they ran in at least one full Campaign run, but not across many.
