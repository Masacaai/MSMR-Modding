# Nexus page: "Known Issues"

- If an enemy is stuck inside a wall or in an unreachable area, move away from him and look elsewhere. He will be teleported out.
- Gadgets still look locked (red, 0/0) after you unlock them. They only look usable once you equip them for the first time.
- If the game loads endlessly after dying at the end of a run, then force-close it and reopen the game. The mod should restore your loadout normally.
