## Manhattan Skies 0.1.0 alpha 1

Change the time of day and the sky of Marvel's Spider-Man Remastered from the game's own Settings menu: Sunrise, 124 skies, sun, fog and haze controls, presets, and a day-night cycle that plays all day on its own.

> **This is an alpha.** It has been played by one person and works, but expect rough edges (see below). Back up your saves before you try it.

### Requirements

- Marvel's Spider-Man Remastered, **`Spider-Man.exe` version exactly 4.0630.0.0**. Any other build: the mod refuses to touch the game. Developed and tested on the Steam version.
- [Overstrike](https://www.nexusmods.com/marvelsspidermanremastered/mods/4199) 1.8.1 with **Enable .script support** and **Add '-scripts' to commandline.txt** turned on.
  Overstrike's stock scripts proxy has a known issue; see the [setup notes](https://github.com/Masacaai/MSMR-Modding/blob/main/docs/GETTING_STARTED.md#2-overstrike-and-the-scripts-proxy).

### Install

1. Download **`ManhattanSkies.script`** below.
2. In Overstrike, add it to your mod list and press **Install mods**.
3. Launch the game, load a save, open the pause menu, then **Settings**: the **MANHATTAN SKIES** tab is after the game's own tabs.

Your settings, presets and saved looks are kept in `Documents\Marvel's Spider-Man Remastered\ManhattanSkies\`, so reinstalling or updating Overstrike mods does not reset them. To remove it: remove the mod in Overstrike and press Install mods again.

### What it does

- **TIME OF DAY**: the game's seven lighting modes, including **Sunrise** (the opening mission's dawn) and the overcast looks its own option never offers. It is remembered across launches, and story missions use it too (the opening mission at night, for example).
- **DYNAMIC CYCLE**: plays a full day over and over (2 to 60 minutes): Sunrise, Day, Sunset, Night. The sun travels smoothly, each phase may use a random sky, one day in four is overcast, and there is no fade to black: the colors shift toward the next sky shortly before it arrives. It pauses with the game.
- **SKY STYLE / SKY VARIANT**: any of the game's **124** skies on top of the time of day (a Sunset sky over Day lighting, a cutscene sky in the open world, and so on).
- **SUN** direction, height, color and brightness; **FOG** density, distance and color; **HAZE**; **EXPOSURE**, **COLOR FILTER**, **CLOUDS**.
- **PRESET**: looks saved as small files in the `Presets` folder (it ships Warm Night, Golden Hour and Dreamy Haze). Copy a file to share a preset; **CUSTOM** keeps your own unsaved settings while a preset is shown.

Press **Y** on the tab to reset the sky rows, **X** to reset one row.

### Known Issues

- **Don't use the game's own time-of-day option while this mod is installed.** The mod owns the time of day: if you change it in the game's Settings, the game loads and the mod then puts the lighting back to what it was playing (or, with the dynamic cycle on, the next phase). Use the TIME OF DAY row on the MANHATTAN SKIES tab instead.
- **Every lighting change stops Spider-Man's momentum in mid-air** (it is the game's own mini-load, with a loading icon and a rebuild of the map markers). The dynamic cycle waits up to 45 seconds for you to slow down before it switches, but if you swing nonstop it will switch anyway.
- The cycle starts about 30 seconds after a world loads and when the screen changes lighting there is a small pop (new sky and lights); the colors blend beforehand to soften it.
- Some skies come from cutscenes and look odd or foggy in the open world (a very short draw distance, a stretched sky). They are offered on purpose.
- A sun color edit can leave tinted patches in cached lighting until the time of day changes.
- The tab only knows the game's own lights: building windows and street lamps follow the lighting mode and cannot be controlled on their own.
- Rain follows the lighting mode (Night and Overcast rain, Day does not); there is no rain switch.
