[size=5][b]Description[/b][/size]
Manhattan Skies lets you change the time of day and the sky of Marvel's Spider-Man Remastered from the game's own Settings menu, whenever you like, without leaving the game.

Pick Early Morning (the opening mission's dawn, which the game never lets you choose), switch to any of the 124 skies that are hidden in the game's files (cutscene skies, mission skies, a gloomy blue hour, a golden sunset), steer the sun, thicken the fog, and save your favorite looks as presets. Or turn on the Dynamic Cycle and watch Manhattan run through a whole day on its own: Early Morning, Day, Sunset, Night, with a different random sky each time, and no black loading screen in between.

[b]This is an alpha.[/b] It has been played a lot by one person and works, but expect rough edges (see Known Issues). Back up your saves before you try it.

[size=5][b]Installation instructions[/b][/size]
[list=1]
[*]Install [url=https://www.nexusmods.com/marvelsspidermanremastered/mods/4199]Overstrike[/url] 1.8.1 and, in its settings, turn on [b]Enable .script support[/b] and [b]Add '-scripts' to commandline.txt[/b].
[*]Download [b]ManhattanSkies.script[/b] and add it to your mod list in Overstrike.
[*]Press [b]Install mods[/b].
[*]Launch the game, load a save, open the pause menu and go to [b]Settings[/b]. The [b]MANHATTAN SKIES[/b] tab is after the game's own tabs.
[/list]
Your settings, presets and saved looks are kept in [i]Documents\Marvel's Spider-Man Remastered\ManhattanSkies\[/i], so reinstalling or updating Overstrike mods does not reset them. To remove the mod, remove it in Overstrike and press Install mods again.

[size=5][b]Main features[/b][/size]
[list]
[*][b]TIME OF DAY[/b]: the game's seven lighting modes, including [b]Early Morning[/b] and the overcast looks the game's own option never offers. The change is remembered between launches, and story missions use it too (the opening mission at night, for example).
[*][b]DYNAMIC CYCLE[/b]: plays a full day over and over, from 2 to 60 minutes per day. The sun travels smoothly across the sky, each phase may use a random sky from a hand-picked list, and about one day in four is overcast. There is no fade to black: the colors shift toward the next sky shortly before it arrives. The cycle pauses with the game, and it waits for a calm moment so the switch does not catch you mid-swing.
[*][b]SKY STYLE and SKY VARIANT[/b]: put any of the game's [b]124 skies[/b] on top of the time of day, for example a sunset sky over daytime lighting.
[*][b]SUN[/b]: direction, height, color and brightness.
[*][b]FOG and HAZE[/b]: density, distance and color of the fog, and a distance haze you can add or remove.
[*][b]EXPOSURE, COLOR FILTER, CLOUDS[/b]: brighten or darken the whole picture, tint it, and switch the cloud layer on or off.
[*][b]PRESETS[/b]: looks are saved as small text files in a Presets folder (it ships Warm Night, Golden Hour and Dreamy Haze). Copy a file to share a preset. [b]CUSTOM[/b] keeps your own unsaved settings while a preset is shown.
[/list]
Every row has a reset button, and there is a reset-all for the tab (see the legend at the bottom of the screen).

[size=5][b]Requirements[/b][/size]
[list]
[*]Marvel's Spider-Man Remastered, [b]Spider-Man.exe version exactly 4.0630.0.0[/b] (right-click the exe, Properties, Details). On any other game build the mod refuses to touch the game. Developed and tested on the Steam version.
[*][url=https://www.nexusmods.com/marvelsspidermanremastered/mods/4199]Overstrike[/url] 1.8.1 with script support and the '-scripts' option turned on (see Installation).
[*]Overstrike's stock scripts proxy has a known issue where it can start game scanning inside helper processes. See the [url=https://github.com/Masacaai/MSMR-Modding/blob/main/docs/GETTING_STARTED.md#2-overstrike-and-the-scripts-proxy]setup notes[/url] for your options.
[*]No other mods are required. It works alongside atmosphere mods: whichever sky mods you have installed replace the game's skies, and Manhattan Skies uses them like the originals.
[/list]

[size=5][b]Known Issues[/b][/size]
[list]
[*][b]Don't use the game's own time-of-day option while this mod is installed.[/b] The mod owns the time of day: if you change it in the game's Settings, the game loads and the mod then puts the lighting back to what it was playing (or, with the Dynamic Cycle on, the next phase). Use the TIME OF DAY row on the MANHATTAN SKIES tab instead.
[*][b]Every lighting change stops Spider-Man's momentum in mid-air.[/b] It is the game's own mini-load, the same one its Settings option uses. The Dynamic Cycle waits up to 45 seconds for you to slow down before it switches, but if you swing nonstop it will switch anyway.
[*]There is a small pop (new sky and lights) when the lighting changes. The colors blend beforehand to soften it, and there is no black screen.
[*]The Dynamic Cycle starts about 30 seconds after a world loads.
[*]Some skies come from cutscenes and look odd or foggy in the open world (a very short draw distance, a stretched sky). They are offered on purpose, so you can try them.
[*]A sun color change can leave tinted patches in cached lighting until the time of day changes.
[*]Building windows and street lamps follow the lighting mode and cannot be controlled on their own. Rain follows the lighting mode too (Night and Overcast rain, Day does not); there is no rain switch.
[*]Installing the package through Overstrike has been tested by one person. Please report any install problem.
[/list]
